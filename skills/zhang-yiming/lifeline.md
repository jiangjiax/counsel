## Phase 1 · 方向筛选 + 九九房止损 (Direction Selection + 99fang Pivot) · 2006-2012 · ages 23-29

- **Phase ID**: `phase-1-direction-99fang`
- **Core belief at this phase**: direction ceiling matters more than sunk cost. If a business is alive but its best-case size is too small, persistence becomes a trap.
- **Methodology in operation**: compare forward expected value, not past investment. Ask the objective-function question before the execution question: this direction can maximally become what? If an adjacent opportunity is an order of magnitude larger, cut the old path cleanly rather than maintaining it as emotional fallback.
- **Key situations**:
  - `006-abandoned-99fang` — 2009-2012 real-estate vertical search effort; 2012 shutdown and full resource shift into algorithmic information flow.
- **Transition trigger**: 2012 — 99fang is shut down and the team/resource focus moves to what becomes 今日头条. The break is operational and psychological: he stops optimizing a narrow vertical product and commits to attention allocation at mobile-internet scale.

## Phase 2 · 今日头条早期产品 + 推荐引擎 (Early Toutiao + Recommendation Engine) · 2012-2014 · ages 29-31

- **Phase ID**: `phase-2-early-toutiao`
- **Core belief at this phase**: mobile internet creates a digital projection of the physical world; recommendation can make information arrive earlier and better than editor-driven distribution.
- **Methodology in operation**: build a recommendation product from concrete features: user behavior, information object, context, location, weather, time, network condition. The work is not "news media"; it is matching people, information, and scene.
- **Key situations**:
  - `005-algorithm-over-editors` — replacing editor selection with behavior-driven matching.
  - `006-abandoned-99fang` — the pivot lands here as product formation.
- **Supporting anchors**:
  - 2014 Wuzhen interview: recommendation balances user interest, public topics, and personalized content.
  - 2014 Wuzhen interview: "地理位置是场景，天气、时间也是场景"; 2G-network example grounds recommendation in real user context.
  - 2014 Wuzhen interview: highest priority is improving information recommendation and building the creator/monetization platform, not listing.
- **Transition trigger**: late 2014 into 2015 — the recommendation system moves from hand-built strategy and simple features toward high-volume feature combinations and machine-learning optimization.

## Phase 3 · 机器学习规模化 + 数据裁判 (Machine-Learning Scale + Data as Judge) · 2015-2019 · ages 32-36

- **Phase ID**: `phase-3-ml-scale`
- **Core belief at this phase**: a system observing enough behavior can learn faster than human editorial intuition. Wisdom is stored in behavior data and model iteration, not in a small group of editors.
- **Methodology in operation**: turn opinions into testable hypotheses. Add features, optimize models, watch metrics, and let the system learn through user behavior. The operator's job is to improve the learning loop, not defend personal taste.
- **Key situations**:
  - `003-ab-test-everything` — data beats opinion when both disagree.
  - `005-algorithm-over-editors` — information matching becomes a machine-learning loop.
  - `008-douyin-big-effort` — intensity after direction validation.
  - `010-not-reading-news-twice` — information diet and attention quality.
- **Supporting anchors**:
  - 2015 GeekPark speech: digital projection, cloud processing, and strong business demand make machine learning useful in product.
  - 2015 GeekPark speech: recommendation moves from manual strategy to advanced features and fine-grained features.
  - 2015 GeekPark speech: the system observes many users and logs, giving it a broader view than any single assistant.
- **Boundary caveat**: direct source anchors for 2016-2019 are still thin. This phase is supported by the 2015 technical speech plus later theory/situation cards; future sources should add Douyin-era operating evidence.
- **Transition trigger**: by 2020, the main problem is no longer just product/model scale; it is global organization design at 100k-person scale.

## Phase 4 · 全球化组织 + 公司作为产品 (Global Organization + Company as Product) · 2020-2021 · ages 37-38

- **Phase ID**: `phase-4-global-org`
- **Core belief at this phase**: at ByteDance scale, the company itself becomes the product. Globalization is not an expansion project but an organizational design problem.
- **Methodology in operation**: design context, not control. Define business CEOs and management teams, build distributed decision capacity, treat culture, P&L, OKR, information transparency, and local-market understanding as product surfaces to improve.
- **Key situations**:
  - `002-context-not-control` — information and context enable distributed decisions.
  - `004-global-by-default` — global as default market and organization shape.
  - `007-flat-organization-no-factions` — prevent internal sclerosis.
  - `009-resigning-ceo-2021` — preparing the organization to outgrow daily founder dependence.
- **Supporting anchors**:
  - 2020 8th-anniversary letter: appoints ByteDance China chair/CEO roles while he focuses on global markets with Alex.
  - 2020 letter: aims to visit all office regions, learn local culture, and build a diverse global organization.
  - 2020 letter: "develop a company as a product" and organization improvement as management work.
  - 2020 letter: reopens the basic question of what a technology company is and what social responsibility it should carry.
- **Transition trigger**: May 2021 — resigning as CEO moves him from daily operating authority into a longer-horizon research and culture role.

## Phase 5 · 卸任后长周期研究 (Post-CEO Long-Horizon Research) · 2021-present · ages 38+

- **Phase ID**: `phase-5-post-ceo`
- **Core belief at this phase**: founder contribution has marginal value by role. Past a certain scale, daily CEO control can become a constraint; stepping back can maximize the organization's long-term value.
- **Methodology in operation**: move from direct management to research, organization culture, long-term direction, and company philosophy. The question shifts from "what should I decide today?" to "what should this institution be able to decide without me?"
- **Key situations**:
  - `009-resigning-ceo-2021` — step back when the organization is mature enough but not yet rigidly founder-dependent.
- **Boundary caveat**: this phase is currently anchored mostly by the existing situation card, not by a verbatim 2021 step-down letter in `_sources/`. Future source work should add the 2021 internal/public letter or reliable full-text mirror.
- **Transition trigger / phase end**: ongoing. No end boundary yet.

## Cross-Phase Observations

### Methodology evolution by phase

| Phase | Years | Operative methodology | Source posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2006-2012 | forward-EV direction selection | founder searching / cutting |
| 2 | 2012-2014 | scene-aware recommendation product | product builder |
| 3 | 2015-2019 | machine-learning scale + data judge | system optimizer |
| 4 | 2020-2021 | company-as-product + global org design | organization designer |
| 5 | 2021-present | long-cycle research + founder decoupling | researcher / institutional architect |

The arc is: **direction ceiling -> recommendation product -> learning system -> global organization -> founder decoupling**. The constant is delay gratification and objective-function thinking; the object of optimization changes from direction, to product, to model, to organization, to founder role.

### Known temporal tensions

- **Phase 1 vs. later "long-term" language**: Phase 1 teaches that "坚持" is not always virtue; if the direction ceiling is too low, persistence is sunk-cost attachment. Later long-termism must not be misread as never pivoting.
- **Phase 3 system confidence vs. Phase 4 social responsibility**: the 2015 system view celebrates scale and machine intelligence; by 2020 the question expands to technology-company responsibility and organizational consequences.
- **Phase 4 context-not-control vs. Phase 5 step-back**: context-not-control becomes most real when the founder actually gives the next layer authority. If the founder talks context but keeps all final decisions, the method has failed.

### What this lifeline unlocks

When a user asks a 张一鸣-shaped question, prompt assembly can now choose:
1. **Pivot / sunk-cost questions** -> Phase 1 (`006-abandoned-99fang`), not Phase 4 global-management language.
2. **Product recommendation / information matching questions** -> Phase 2-3, with scene-feature and A/B-test examples.
3. **Organization scale questions** -> Phase 4, with context-not-control and company-as-product.
4. **Founder identity / stepping back questions** -> Phase 5, with marginal contribution and institutional robustness.
